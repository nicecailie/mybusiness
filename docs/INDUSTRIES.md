## Implemented shared and specialist flows

- A: single purchase form, inline supplier, partial receiving with receipt-backed bills, supplier payments/history, running/equipment/other spending, journal-based Money and scoped navigation.
- B: child variant SKUs/barcodes with independent shared stock/cost; optional branch lots with FEFO/expired blocking; transfer/return lot snapshots; low-stock purchase prefill; optional inclusive VAT; net-of-tax product/category profit.
- C: New/Preparing/Ready/Served/Paid and cancellation; table append; money-free kitchen API; waiter/kitchen roles; captured recipe consumption and historical-cost returns; shared price-checked, idempotent postSale settlement.
- D: guest reservations, overlap locks/database guard, check-in, service-product invoices at checkout, deposit liability/application/refund, partial/full FinancePayment, restaurant charge clearing without duplicate income, housekeeping and repair status. Receptionist and housekeeping permissions exclude general finance/suppliers/team.

## Boundaries

Expiry-tracked products require online sale; ordinary and variant products retain offline replay. Recipes settle through restaurant/POS rather than independent credit invoices. Rates are inclusive and VAT defaults to zero; no statutory tax rate is invented. Room revenue uses booked nights/rate at checkout; restaurant room-charge revenue is recognized at charge and only cleared at checkout. Deposits are held liabilities until applied; cancellation/no-show refunds them rather than forfeiting them. POS drawers and hotel FinancePayment cash are distinct sources.

Advanced bar tabs, serving-unit recipes, deposit forfeiture, early-departure proration, guest credit notes, depreciation and unrelated specialist industries remain deferred. Room-charge POS refunds are explicitly blocked pending guest-credit review. Legacy stays are flagged unbilled and are not silently booked into historical revenue. See [implementation report](INDUSTRY_WORKSPACE_IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT.md) for exact assumptions and tests.

Onboarding still stores the chosen industry. Existing industry changes are not accepted by the business-update contract, protecting historical operational data. Production privileged users retain the existing MFA requirement. Fictional demo accounts require the explicitly enabled demo seed and ignored local credentials.
